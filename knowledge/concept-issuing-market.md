# Issuing market and headquarters

The issuing market selects the fictional product configuration. Meridian Commercial Property v3 supports the German issuing market, EUR currency, and insured risks in Germany or Italy.

Customer headquarters describes the organization. The standard synthetic manufacturer is headquartered in Germany. Moving Warehouse to Italy preserves the customer's German headquarters, the German issuing market, and Factory in Germany.

These are invented rules for this demonstration, not jurisdictional guidance.
